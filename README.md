# ♻️ Eco Cycle - Plastic Bottle Recycling Management System

## 🌍 Overview

Eco Cycle is a full-stack web application designed to address the growing issue of plastic bottle pollution by promoting responsible recycling through a digital reward-based system.

The platform enables users to recycle plastic bottles through authorized collection centers, earn reward points based on their contributions, and redeem those points for eco-friendly products manufactured from recycled materials. By integrating technology with environmental sustainability, Eco Cycle encourages citizens to actively participate in Sri Lanka's circular economy.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Promote responsible plastic bottle recycling.
* Digitize the recycling process for improved transparency.
* Reward users for environmental contributions.
* Track plastic waste from collection to production.
* Support sustainable product manufacturing using recycled materials.

## 🌱 Impact

Eco Cycle transforms plastic waste into valuable resources by connecting citizens, collectors, transport teams, inventory facilities, production units, and finance departments through a single digital ecosystem.

The system promotes environmental sustainability, community engagement, and circular economic growth while helping reduce plastic pollution.
